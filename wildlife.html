Uganda's wildlife

Uganda is filled with an number of insects,birds and animals (fauna) and also plants (flora) that are pleasant to view for all kinds of tourists. to look at as a tourist.Uganda is a country with wondeful people who stop at nothing to see that wildlife is protected and conserved in any possible way.And that is why Uganda is filled with a number of national parks,game reserves,game sanctuaries,zoos and many others.

National Parks

National Parks are large government protected areas designed to conserve natural landscapes,ecosystems and wildlife, while often allowing pubic enjoyment and education.National parks are ten(10)in Uganda and are natural park designated for conservation purposes because of unparalleled national natural,historic or cultural significance.National parks in Uganda include Queen Elizabeth national park,Mgahinga National park,Murchison Falls national park and others.

Game reserves

Game reserves are protected areas of land designated for wildlife conservation,habitat preservation and ecological management, often allowing controlled tourism and hunting.They serve as safe sanctuariesfor endangered species while balancing biodiversity protection with sustainable human interaction and frequently,local community development.These are different from national parks because they are protected areas, often privately owned or managed, that may allow for flexible management,limited hunting and offer a more exclusive,less crowded experience than national parks.

Game sanctuaries

Game sanctuaries are protected areas where animals can safely live without being unduly disturbed by poachers,hunters, or other intruders. There are thirteen designated sanctuaries in Uganda, which form part of the country's protected areas aimed at conserving habitats and species.Game sanctuaries in Uganda include Entebbe Wildlife sanctuary,Ngamba island chimpanzee sanctuary and Ziwa Rhino sanctuary.

Zoos

Zoos are specialized facilities for exhibiting,conserving and breeding wildlife often functioning asrescue and rehabilitation centers for endangered species.Modern zoos prioritize naturalistic habitats over cages to educate visitors while providing safe spaces for endangered creatures. Popular examples include the Uganda Wildlife Conservation Education Centre (Entebbe Zoo). Another kind of the category for zoos are aquariums.
